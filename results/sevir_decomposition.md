## Headline cell (POOL16-max, threshold 219)

- deterministic backbone: **0.1468** (frequency bias 0.172)
- + cascade: **0.3069** (bias 1.037) -> **+109.1%**
- backbone + best per-cell knob: **0.3201**  (beats the cascade)
  - cascade + same: **0.3025**; residual cascade gain **-5.5%**
- backbone + single global quantile map: **0.2638**
  - cascade + same: **0.2978**; residual cascade gain **+12.9%**

The cascade raises frequency bias from 0.172 to 1.037, i.e. its mechanical effect at this operating point is to move the model toward calibration. That is what a free monotone knob also does, which is why the two are largely non-additive.
